## 🕹️ How To Play

### Single Player

1. Choose **HEADS** or **TAILS**
2. Click **🪙 FLIP IT**
3. Wait for the coin to land
4. Match the result to win
5. Try to build your winning streak 🔥

### Two Player Mode

1. Click **👥 Add 2 Players**
2. Enter both player names
3. Choose Heads or Tails
4. Flip the coin
5. The player whose side appears wins the toss 🏆
